\# Month-1 Summary

**Question**: Where is most suitable to establish new primary health centers in Sapele LGA?

**Operation**: I ran a buffer analysis using a 1km radius.

**Rationale for the Operation**: I chose this analysis so it would show the coverage extent of the already existing PHCs in the LGA. This would then be foundational step in deciding where to site new ones.

**Expectation**: I expected to get back 14 PHCs with a 1km buffer around them.

**Results**: I got back 14 PHC locations with their 1km radius buffer circle around them.
**Surprises**: The most surprising knowledge from this is the fact that there are quite large areas in the urban areas that are not covered by any PHC.

**Needed Data**: I don't think I need any new data. Although, I have some data in my possession that I have not yet utilized, but will definitely play an important role down the line in the course of answering this question.
